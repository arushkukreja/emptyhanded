## Intent

A cinematic 30-second launch promo that tells one believable story: Alex has seven days until Maya's birthday, EmptyHanded remembers the occasion, surfaces picks that fit her, and turns the deadline into a warm, confident gift moment. The story should mix human emotion with the real product instead of becoming a feature tour.

## Assets

- http://localhost:3000/dashboard — signed-in Alex Morgan dashboard and Maya's seven-day occasion.
- http://localhost:3000/recommendations — real live-catalog recommendations and product imagery.
- http://localhost:3000/email — the reminder email preview.
- public/images — existing recipient portraits and product images already used by the app.

## Customizations

- Feature the site's own captured screens as the product proof.
- Use a cinematic countdown motif, warm editorial pacing, and restrained UI camera moves.
- Keep the project reusable in Remotion while HyperFrames owns capture, story direction, motion language, and verification.

## Notes

- Use real catalog product images, never generic stock products.
- Keep the emotional center on noticing Maya, not on generic productivity software.
- Avoid a conventional blue SaaS launch aesthetic; preserve EmptyHanded's cream, navy, orange, serif-led brand.
