## Video direction

- Keep every scene visually quiet and centered on one action.
- Never animate two elements at the same time inside a frame.
- Use hard cuts between frames so scene transitions do not compete with the focal animation.
- No subtitles, narration, browser chrome, decorative copy, secondary callouts, or late camera drift.
- Fraunces carries display numerals and the lowercase `emptyhanded.` logo; Plus Jakarta Sans carries labels and interface details.
